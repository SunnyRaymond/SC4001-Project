We used Claude Code, ChatGPT and Codex to assist with literature discovery, refinement of the research questions and experimental plan, code generation and debugging, running the experimental pipeline, analysis and visualization of saved results, and drafting and editing this report. This assistance included preparing Python and job scripts, suggesting interpretations of the results, and producing LaTeX text, tables and figures. The repository README records the stages of AI use.

We reran the main experiments and checked the resulting measurements. Verification also included checking cited claims against the original sources, comparing the implementation with the specified methods and configurations, and checking reported values against saved experimental outputs. The reported measurements come from runs of the experimental code.

The team reviewed AI suggestions and made the final decisions about the scope, methods, interpretation and presentation. We accept responsibility for the accuracy and integrity of the report and accompanying code, including material prepared with AI assistance.
